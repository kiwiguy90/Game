# Blackthorn Manor — next time

Ideas for the next sessions, best first inside each section. Tick them off
(`[x]`) as they land. Everything here should keep to the game's rules: no
combat, nothing chases you, light is the reward, and the house is the
character.

Already in the game (so not on this list): the storm trips MAIN once with a
lightning strike; fireplaces and candles; the pause menu's mouse
sensitivity, invert, volume, field of view, graphics and brightness;
weather and time of day; the map; the haunt that undoes your lights.

## Start here (the recommended first three)

- [ ] **Dimmers and lamps you switch on yourself.** A dimmer knob in the
      drawing room, dining room, library and master bedroom (scroll the mouse
      or hold E to turn it); table, desk and bedside lamps each on their own
      little switch, so a room can be lit a lamp at a time.
- [ ] **The storm takes the power circuit by circuit.** Instead of one trip:
      lightning can knock out a single circuit (the upper floor, the
      kitchen), the lights brown out and flicker first, and you reset each
      breaker in the cellar board by torchlight. Lights come back one circuit
      at a time, with a different sound for each.
- [ ] **Key remapping and a controls screen.** Change any key in the pause
      menu, with a reset to defaults.

## Light (the heart of it)

- [ ] **Torch batteries.** The torch slowly dims; spare batteries in drawers
      and the garage. Optional, with a setting to turn it off.
- [ ] **Matches that run out.** A box with a count; more in the kitchen and
      the lodge.
- [ ] **The house from outside.** Make lit rooms glow more strongly in their
      windows from the drive, the lake and the folly, so you can see your
      progress from across the estate.
- [ ] **Light switch sounds by type.** Old brass toggles, Bakelite rockers,
      pull cords in the bathrooms and the larder, a rotary switch in the
      cellar.
- [ ] **Bulbs you replace.** Blown bulbs stay dark until you fit a new one
      from the box in the pantry.
- [ ] **Chandeliers that sway** in the hall and the ballroom when the wind
      gets in or the front doors open.
- [ ] **Christmas lights.** A box in the attic; string them along the porch
      or the stables.
- [ ] **A light-show moment.** With every estate group lit, the whole place
      on at once from the folly tower: one of the best views in the game.

## New places and things

- [ ] **A security room in the lodge.** A bank of old CCTV screens (drive,
      gate, pond, hall). You see a light go off in a room you are not in.
- [ ] **A front-door intercom to the lodge.** It works with the doorbells;
      once, something answers.
- [ ] **A music room.** A gramophone with records you choose and play
      anywhere in the house; the dust-sheeted piano made playable (the
      theme, if you find the sheet music).
- [ ] **A cinema room** with a projector and a reel of the family's home
      films (a short silent scene told in still frames).
- [ ] **A stone bridge over a stream** with lights under its arch.
- [ ] **A hidden room behind the cellar wine racks.**
- [ ] **A walled vegetable garden and fruit trees** past the courtyard's orchard
      gate, with a potting shed.
- [ ] **A pavilion by the tennis court**, with its own lights.
- [ ] **A secret passage** from the library to the old servants' stair.
- [ ] **A dumb waiter** (a small food lift, separate from the cage lift)
      between the kitchen and the dining room that sometimes comes up with
      something in it.

## Atmosphere and scares (all non-violent)

- [ ] **The phone rings.** Once the lodge line is fixed, the phone in the
      hall rings at 3 a.m.
- [ ] **A 1974 broadcast** that the radiogram picks up once, late at night.
- [ ] **Moving portraits.** A painting is turned to the wall the next time
      you pass.
- [ ] **Breath in the cold rooms.** You see your breath in the ice house,
      the cellar and the attic.
- [ ] **Animals.** A cat in the stables that follows you; moths around the
      lit lamps; frogs at the pond at night.
- [ ] **Wind you can hear room by room**, and doors that creak when the
      wind changes.
- [ ] **Seasons.** Autumn leaves, a snow night with footprints in the snow
      (and once, footprints that are not yours).

## Story

- [ ] **An opening scene** that sets up who you are and why you inherited
      the house.
- [ ] **Three nights as three chapters**, each telling more of Eleanor's
      story, with a title card at the start of each.
- [ ] **More readables:** letters from the doctor, the gardener's notebook,
      the cook's order book, school reports.
- [ ] **A second ending** for finding everything (all clues, all lights, the
      ice-house secret).
- [ ] **A voice for Eleanor:** a few whispered lines, recorded or
      synthesised, only at key moments.

## Polish for selling it

- [ ] **A list of things to find** (lights lit, clues found, places visited)
      on the pause screen, and a percentage on the ending screen.
- [ ] **Achievements**, such as "Light every room", "Ring both bells",
      "Find the ice-house secret".
- [ ] **Save slots** instead of one save, with the date and the room you
      saved in.
- [ ] **Subtitles and text size settings.**
- [ ] **A photo mode:** pause, hide the interface, move the camera, save a
      screenshot.
- [ ] **A loading screen** with a tip or a line from the journal.
- [ ] **Performance pass:** a "low" graphics preset for older laptops, and
      check frame rate across the whole estate.
- [ ] **A trailer route:** a scripted fly-through of the best views to
      record a trailer and store screenshots.
- [ ] **Packaging** as a desktop app (Windows and Mac) for selling on a
      store such as itch.io or Steam.

## More ideas (second batch)

### Lights and power to play with

- [ ] **A diesel generator** in an outbuilding: pull-start it, it coughs and
      catches, and the estate runs on it when the mains is down. It runs
      out of fuel unless you fill it.
- [ ] **A lighting timer** in the lodge: set groups to come on at dusk and go
      off at dawn by themselves, with an old clockwork time-switch dial.
- [ ] **Coloured lights:** a gel box in the attic to change the floodlights
      to red, green, blue or amber.
- [ ] **A light-show mode:** at the lodge panel, record a sequence of
      switches and play it back in time with the radio.
- [ ] **Motion-sensor lights** on the garage and the stables that click on
      when something walks past, even when you are not there.
- [ ] **Search beam on the folly tower** you can sweep across the lake and
      the woods by hand.
- [ ] **Fairy lights in the trees** along the drive, and floating lanterns
      on the lake.
- [ ] **A power meter** in the cellar whose needle swings as you switch more
      of the house on, with a way to overload it.

### New places

- [ ] **An observatory** on the roof or the hill, with a telescope that
      shows the moon and the stars (and once, a lit window that should be
      dark).
- [ ] **A treehouse** in the woods, Eleanor's, with her drawings pinned up.
- [ ] **A doll's house of the manor** in the nursery whose tiny lights copy
      the real ones you have switched on.
- [ ] **A darkroom** with a red safelight and photographs that develop as you
      watch.
- [ ] **A gazebo / bandstand** on the lawn, strung with bulbs.
- [ ] **A dovecote, kennels and beehives** for a working-estate feel.
- [ ] **A windmill or water pump** by the lake that once made the estate's
      power.
- [ ] **A train halt** at the end of a disused line through the woods, with
      a signal lamp you can light.

### Things to do

- [ ] **Puzzles:** a wall safe with a combination found in letters, a
      fuse-box puzzle in the cellar, a hidden switch behind a painting.
- [ ] **Keys:** a ring of keys in the lodge, labelled rooms kept locked
      until you find the right key.
- [ ] **A camera:** take photographs with an old camera and pin them up in
      the lodge; sometimes the photo shows something you did not see.
- [ ] **Fishing from the jetty** and **rowing the boat** yourself.
- [ ] **Fill the fountain and the pool** by turning on the pumps.
- [ ] **A gramophone party:** light the ballroom, wind the gramophone and the
      room seems to fill with dancers for a moment.
- [ ] **Fireworks** in the boathouse for the end of the game.

### Ways to play

- [ ] **"Caretaker" mode:** no story or scares, just the estate to explore
      and light at your own pace.
- [ ] **Speedrun timer** for getting out, with a best time.
- [ ] **Daily challenge:** a random set of lights to switch on in the
      fewest steps.
- [ ] **A hard setting:** fewer matches, the torch runs down, the haunt more
      often.

### Sound and music

- [ ] **More music:** a quiet piece for each night, a music-box tune in the
      nursery, the theme on the playable piano.
- [ ] **Room acoustics:** echoes in the hall and the cellar, muffled sound in
      the bedrooms, a hollow sound in the ice house.
- [ ] **A sound for every switch type** (see the Light section above).

### Selling and sharing

- [ ] **A store page kit:** screenshots, a short trailer, a description and
      a capsule image.
- [ ] **A free demo** of the first night (the drive, the lodge and the
      ground floor).
- [ ] **A feedback button** that copies your settings and position to the
      clipboard to paste into a bug report.
- [ ] **A credits screen** and a version number on the title screen.
