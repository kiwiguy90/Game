# Blackthorn Manor — next time

Ideas for the next sessions. Tick them off (`[x]`) as they land.

## The goal of the game

1. Your car breaks down at the gates.
2. You go inside, and get locked in — trapped in the grounds and the house.
3. You explore and find out the story.
4. You unlock areas as you go.
5. You get out, and drive away.

Already in: the gates shut and bolt behind you on the drive; the gate motor
switch in the cellar lets you out; the spare wheel and the battery come from
the garage.

## Keep it easy

The game should not get too hard. For anything we add:

- Only a few things are needed to get out. Everything else is optional,
  for players who want to explore.
- Things you need are kept close to where you need them, or in places the
  game points you to (the phone's objective, a note, the house plan).
- Hints: if you are stuck for a while, the objective gives a clearer clue.
- No running out of anything you need to finish the game.

## To do

- [ ] **The house traps you.** Once you are inside, the front doors shut and
      lock behind you, and the house does not want you to leave. You have to
      find what opens the doors and the way back out to the grounds. (Our
      own house and story, not copied from any film or book. The house
      changing and growing is under "Maybe later".)
- [ ] **The cellar is flooded.** The storm has flooded the cellar, and the
      gate switch you need to get out is down there. You go down the steps
      with the torch and stop at the water's edge. To get in, you turn on the
      power to the pumps; they thump into life and the water drains away
      slowly, until you can walk down there. Things the water carried are
      left on the floor as it goes down (letters, a doll, a chair), and some
      can be picked up and read.
- [ ] **The house floods on the storm night.** Only on the storm night: water
      comes up from the cellar, then across the floors and the grounds alike,
      slowly to the waist and then faster, floor by floor, until it stands at
      the top of the mansion's roof. Only the towers and the clock stand above
      it. As it climbs it gets into the wiring: every light, inside and out,
      flickers, crackles and zaps, then sparks and goes out — some with a bang
      — lowest first on every floor. Fires hiss out. You wade, then swim (on
      the surface or under it; you cannot drown), and you can go in and out of
      the house. When the pumps run, the water goes down and each light dries
      out, comes back on and is sound again.
      *Test run built: F9 starts the flood, F9 again runs the pumps. Still to
      do: start it by itself on the storm night.*
- [ ] **Dimmer switches.** Turn the lights up and down in the main rooms,
      not only on and off.
- [ ] **The storm cuts one part of the house at a time — rarely.** Not every
      storm, and not often: the lights flicker first, then one floor or
      wing goes dark, and you reset that breaker at the fuse box in the
      cellar.
- [ ] **A doll's house of the manor** in the nursery. Its tiny lights copy
      the real ones you have switched on — and once, one lights up in a room
      you have not been to.
- [ ] **The hall phone rings at 3 a.m.** Nobody should be calling, and the
      line is supposed to be dead.
- [ ] **Keys hidden around the house.** Some doors start locked; their keys
      are in drawers, coat pockets and on hooks, each with a tag saying
      which door it opens.
- [ ] **A jack and a wheel brace to find.** Changing the tyre needs both, and
      they are kept in different places (the garage, the stables). At the
      moment the jack is in your car's boot; this changes that.
- [ ] **The garage is locked.** Its key is somewhere in the house, so you
      have to find it before you can get the tyre and the battery.
- [ ] **A plan of the house** in the study, showing where things are kept,
      with Eleanor's notes in the margins about what is hidden.

- [ ] **Snow.** A new weather: snow falls and settles white on the lawns, roofs
      and drive; your footprints follow you; lit windows glow on the snow.
- [ ] **Time passes by itself.** Night slowly turns to dawn, day, dusk and back
      to night; the outside lights fade as the sun comes up.
- [ ] **Fountains and water features on the panel.** The garden fountain, the
      pond waterfall and the pool jets on and off, with their sound. And the
      pool water should flow and move better.
- [ ] **Fireworks over the lake**, from the panel, lighting the water, the house
      and the towers in colour.

## Maybe later

Ideas to look at further down the track, not now.

- Coloured floodlights: a panel row to change the house and tower floodlights
  to red, blue, green, purple or warm white.

- The house changes: doors that lead somewhere different, a corridor that
  grows, a room that was not there before, stairs that bring you back to
  the same floor, windows that show the wrong view, a map that stops
  matching, and lighting rooms to calm the house.
